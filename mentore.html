Mentore

Product UI/UX Motion Graphics

Mentore is a productivity wearable start-up used on the frontline that transitioned from proprietary hardware into SaaS.

ROLE

Product Designer
Guro Design Contract

Deliverables

The new iteration of the product was compatible with samsung galaxy watches paired with a mobile and web app.

The wearable

Used by frontline workers in factory environments like Rivian and Johnsonville, the wearable app was used to monitor ergo safety standards, provide productivity feedback and act as a communication vessel between co-workers and management. In some frontline environments the wearable would stay at the workplace and be shared by employees. The companion mobile app would be offered to accomodate access to the app and allow workers to view data in a friendier screen format.
