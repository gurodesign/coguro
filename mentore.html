Overview

The new version of the product was built for samsung galaxy watches and paired with a mobile app with analytics served through a web application. Working with a remote dev team I designed the wearable and mobile interface alongside the analytics web application.

The wearable

Used by frontline workers in factory environments like Rivian and Johnsonville, the wearable app monitored ergo safety standards, provided productivity feedback and acted as a communication vessel between co-workers and management. In some frontline environments the wearable would stay at the workplace and be shared by employees. The companion mobile app would be offered to accomodate access to the app and allow workers to view data in a friendier screen format and make the data accessible even after users returned their wearables. Additionally assigning devices to workers necessitated an intuitive and easy login solution which meant leveraging the paired mobile app to log in as input on a smart watch could be a challenge.

Data visualization

The User Admin

The user admin would be used by managers and executives to track and analyze data both detailed and aggregate. The focus of the dashboard here was to highlight potential safety issues, outliers in performance and minimizing ergo risk factors. We wanted to reinforce the brand and technology, so while the admin was white labeled we would subtly co-brand Mentore in the header.
