OVERVIEW

Tap District gave me a chance to work in a few areas that I hadn't previously had experience with; consumables, inventory systems, and last mile delivery. Collaborating with former bus-dev and engineering co-workers allowed me to dive in and focus on design and product vision.

While providing a functional concept was the priority, we needed a logo quickly and incoporating the spigot mark into the typeform proved to be a quick win.

Disrupting the space doing triple duty

Offering a hollistic sales solution to craft beverage makers represented a unique challenge that bred some unique functionality. Rapid iteration spawned a clean aesthetic, moderate color usage and clever detailing.

Discovery & Purchase

Selling alcohol-based beverages presents a unique challenge as there are a multitude of federal and state laws with significant variations across different jurisdictions. Our goal was to focus on craft makers selling in their local area. We had a relationship with Zilker Brewing in Austin, Texas that would serve as our geolocal launchpad.

The main storefront would showcase all the local sellers in the region, while each distinct seller would have a landing page that could be white labeled with custom branding. Sponsored placements on the homepage would allow for additional revenue streams.

One interesting product detail that I wanted to highlight was the color grading of the beverages. This would give buyers a visual cue as to what to expect from a beer and potentially wine or spirit. The SRM (Standard Reference Method) is a scale used to measure the color of beer. It ranges from 1 (pale straw) to 40 (black), and I created a swatch for each value to visually represent the range helping buyers further their understanding of color and flavor.
