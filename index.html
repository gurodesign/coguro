I'm a multi-disciplinary designer with over 25 years experience in a range of different fields and industries. For the past 5 years I've been focused on operating Guro Design, serving clients from start-up to corporate. In between I've led a design team at CBS Interactive, co-founded a video-based start-up, and headed design at a AAA live experience company. I can provide any design need at any level, and my experience and technical skillset allow me to build products, soup to nuts. I'm blessed to have been able to collaborate with so many amazing people and brands over the years and look forward to connecting with you.
