More than just a designerd
I've been doing this for a while, but I'm still learning something new everyday. I started as a spot illustrator in a mid-sized print shop and I've been operating my own design studio serving brands from start-up to large corp for over 4 years. In between I've led a design team at CBS Interactive, co-founded a video-based start-up, and headed design at a AAA live experience company. I enjoy problem solving using a variety of tools and design methodologies, and working with people in all types of industries. I'm blessed to have been able to collaborate with so many amazing people and brands over the years and look forward to connecting with you.
